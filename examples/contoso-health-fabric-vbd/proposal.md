# Contoso Health — Microsoft Fabric Value-Based Delivery

**Prepared by:** Ines Lantero Oraa · **Date:** 15 Sep 2026 · **Duration:** 3 days (hybrid) · **Attendees:** 12 (data engineers, BI analysts, data architects) · **Fabric release pinned:** 2026-09

## Customer context

- **Industry:** Healthcare payer — regulated, PHI-sensitive.
- **Current stack:** Synapse dedicated pool, ADF, PBI Premium, on-prem SQL.
- **Sensitivity:** Confidential — synthetic data will be used throughout to keep the workshop out of PHI scope.

## Objectives (from scoping call 10 Sep 2026)

- Migrate the legacy on-prem claims warehouse to a Fabric lakehouse (medallion).
- Enable near-real-time fraud alerts on the claims stream.
- Empower analysts with Direct Lake Power BI over the same lakehouse.
- Establish a shared Data Science workspace for readmission risk models.

## Recommended modules

- **Foundation — 3h.** Ground the group in workspaces, OneLake, capacity, and how OneLake maps to their existing ADLS Gen2 estate via shortcuts. [Learn: OneLake overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/onelake/onelake-overview)
- **Lakehouse — 5h.** Bronze/silver/gold on de-identified claims; a clean migration path off the Synapse dedicated pool. [Learn: Lakehouse overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/data-engineering/lakehouse-overview)
- **Data Warehouse — 3h.** Serve gold aggregates via T-SQL to finance's existing tooling; keep SQL surface familiar. [Learn: Fabric Data Warehouse](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/data-warehouse/data-warehousing)
- **Real-Time Intelligence — 5h.** Eventstream + KQL DB + Activator for the fraud alert pipeline — the single highest-value objective. [Learn: RTI overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/real-time-intelligence/overview)
- **Data Science — 4h.** Build and register a readmission-risk model with MLflow; batch score against gold layer. [Learn: Data Science overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/data-science/data-science-overview)

### Modules deliberately excluded

- **Data Factory** — Contoso already has strong ADF expertise; migration angle covered inside Lakehouse lab.
- **Security & Governance** — Regulated-industry deep-dive better handled as a follow-on VBD; we'll cover PHI handling as a lightning talk on Day 3.
- **Power BI** — Direct Lake is demonstrated in-line during Lakehouse and Warehouse labs; Contoso runs a mature PBI CoE so a full lab would repeat internal training.

## Data strategy
